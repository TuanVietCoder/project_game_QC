## **🎯 TIPS FOR HIGHER RESPONSE RATE**

1. **Personalize emails:**
   - Mention specific content they created
   - Show you've actually visited their site/channel
   - Add personal touch (not template copy-paste)

2. **Best timing:**
   - Send Tue-Thu morning (higher open rate)
   - Avoid Monday morning (inbox overload)
   - Avoid weekends

3. **Follow-up:**
   - First email → Wait 5-7 days
   - Second email → "Just checking in..."
   - Third email → Skip (move on to next target)

4. **Subject line tips:**
   - Under 50 characters
   - Avoid spam triggers: "FREE", "EASY MONEY"
   - Use curiosity: "Indie game your audience will love"
