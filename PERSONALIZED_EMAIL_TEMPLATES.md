## **🚀 TIPS GỬI EMAIL**

### **Timing Tối Ưu:**
- ✅ **Tốt nhất:** Thứ 3-4 buổi sáng (9-11 AM)
- ❌ **Tránh:** Thứ 2 buổi sáng (inbox overload)
- ❌ **Tránh:** Thứ 6-7 (low response)
- ❌ **Tránh:** Late night (ít xem email)

### **Subject Line Tips:**
```
✅ Tốt: "Free Game for Review - Ashfall (Soulslike)"
✅ Tốt: "Vietnamese Indie Game - Your Audience Will Love This"
✅ Tốt: "New Game Opportunity - Ashfall"

❌ Tránh: "FREE!!!" (spam trigger)
❌ Tránh: "URGENT" (spam trigger)
❌ Tránh: "Hey" (too casual)
```

### **Response Rate Tips:**
1. **Personalize** - Mention specific content
2. **Short** - Keep under 150 words
3. **Value** - Show why it benefits THEM
4. **Clear CTA** - "Interested?" is good
5. **Signature** - Include name + contact

### **Follow-up Strategy:**
```
Email 1: Initial outreach
↓ (Wait 7 days)
Email 2: "Just checking in..." (brief)
↓ (Wait 7 days)
Email 3: Skip - move to next target
```
