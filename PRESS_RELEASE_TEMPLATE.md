## **FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

### **TuanViet Studio Launches Ashfall: The Ultimate Skill-Based Soulslike Mobile Game**

**Vietnamese Indie Developer Brings Challenging, Award-Worthy Gameplay to iOS & Android**

**Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam** – September 1, 2024 – TuanViet Studio today announced the launch of **Ashfall** (Vietnamese title: Tro Tàn), a groundbreaking pixel-art soulslike mobile game that's revolutionizing the way players experience challenging combat on mobile devices.

---

## **About Ashfall**

Ashfall is a skill-based action RPG that combines:

- **Pure Skill Gameplay:** No stat grinding, no pay-to-win mechanics – only pure player skill determines victory
- **Boss-Focused Combat:** Epic challenging boss battles with learnable patterns and strategic depth
- **Pixel Art Excellence:** Stunning 2D sprite work paired with modern visual effects
- **Multiple Playstyles:** 3 unique weapons offering different combat approaches

**Game Website:** https://ashfall.io.vn

---

## **Key Features**

✨ **Pattern Recognition System**
Players must learn and master boss attack patterns to defeat increasingly challenging enemies. This creates an engaging learning curve that rewards player improvement.

⚔️ **Three Unique Weapons**
- Sword (balanced, safe)
- Spear (fast, medium damage)
- Halberd (high damage, risky)

🎨 **Visual Masterpiece**
Pixel-art graphics that honor retro gaming while incorporating modern techniques and atmospheric effects.

🇻🇳 **Vietnamese Pride**
Developed by TuanViet Studio, a Vietnamese indie team passionate about creating world-class gaming experiences.

📱 **Mobile Optimized**
Available on iOS and Android with optimized touchscreen controls.

## **Why Ashfall Matters**

In an era of casual mobile games dominated by monetization strategies, Ashfall stands apart by:
